Crucial Flood Survival Instructions

Keep these protocols in mind even when cellular network is weak.

🚫 Turn Around, Don't Drown

Never attempt to cross flooded roadways, bridges, or culverts on foot or in vehicles. Water depth and currents are deceptively powerful.

⚡ Disconnect Electricity

Turn off your main circuit breaker and gas line before water enters the premises. Never touch fallen electric wires or submerged power meters.

🎒 Emergency Grab Bag

Pack essential medicines, water bottles, non-perishable biscuits, flashlight, power bank, and identity cards in waterproof zip bags.
